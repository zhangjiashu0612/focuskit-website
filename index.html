The frame

Everything is inside the temples.

Nothing on your face says device. Touch a drop to see what's there.

Drag to turn · touch a drop

Reading contact

A flat metal pad on the inside of each temple, where the frame rests against your skin. It reads. It never stimulates.

Reference contact

A second, smaller pad behind the ear completes the circuit.

The push

A soft pulse at the temple, 150 milliseconds long. Not a buzz, not a notification. A touch that says come back.

The tip

Radio, battery and the decision all live here. Your signal is read on the frame and never leaves it.

Nose pads

Metal, and part of the circuit.

Acetate

Matte, 148 mm temples, a 6° wrap. Takes prescription lenses, or none.
